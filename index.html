Foley Auctioneers is delighted to bring to the the market, this superb four bedroom detached residence, set in the much sought-after development of Seachnall Abbey, offering an exceptional opportunity to acquire a stylish and spacious family home in one of Dunshaughlin’s most desirable locations. Ideally positioned within minutes’ walk of the vibrant main street, the property combines a peaceful residential setting with unrivaled convenience.

From the moment you arrive, the home makes an excellent impression, with its attractive red brick façade and paved driveway.

Internally, the property offers generous, well-proportioned living space designed with modern family life in mind. Bright and welcoming reception areas provide an ideal setting for both everyday living and entertaining and the accommodation is complemented by a number of well-appointed bedrooms, with one en-suite & a family bathroom.

Outside, the property benefits from a private rear garden with patio area and garden shed. It's end of row location and South West orientation help to maximise sunlight.

Location is one of this home’s greatest attributes. Seachnall Abbey is a highly regarded development, while Dunshaughlin itself offers an excellent range of amenities including shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, sporting facilities and essential services. The property is also ideally situated for commuters, with convenient access to Dublin and surrounding areas via motorway networks & bus links.

Combining superb accommodation, an enviable location and the appeal of a detached family home, this property represents an excellent opportunity for buyers seeking quality, convenience and a strong sense of community.

Early viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all this wonderful home has to offer.
